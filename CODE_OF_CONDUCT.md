# Code of Conduct

Sonora welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience. Treat people with respect and help keep the project useful and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code constructively. Explain disagreements without personal attacks.
- Be patient with questions and mistakes. Give feedback that helps someone make progress.
- Respect different experiences, communication styles, and accessibility needs.
- Protect people's privacy. Ask before sharing their personal information, private messages, documents, or recordings.
- Accept reasonable moderation decisions and help resolve conflicts.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content directed at participants, deliberate intimidation, repeated unwanted contact, and publishing someone's private information without permission are unacceptable. Spam and deliberate disruption are also grounds for moderation.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, Discussions, and other spaces where someone represents Sonora.

For a public thread that needs moderation, tag the repository maintainer and describe the behavior calmly. Avoid reposting sensitive content. For sensitive incidents or abuse, use GitHub's [reporting controls](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/reporting-abuse-or-spam); reports submitted to GitHub are handled by GitHub.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, lock a thread, warn a participant, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. The response depends on the severity, context, and whether behavior is repeated. Serious harassment or threats may result in immediate restrictions.

This policy applies equally to users, contributors, and maintainers.
